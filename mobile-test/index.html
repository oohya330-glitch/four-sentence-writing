根據前三句，哪一個結果最合理？

第5回｜四句話寫作

比較並做出選擇

選項 → 比較 → 選擇＋理由 → 具體例子

第 1 頁／共 3 頁
本頁題目如果班級要安排校外教學，你會選擇參觀美術館，還是前往遊樂園？
任務 1｜提出兩個選項

這一句主要告訴讀者什麼？

先找出句子中提到的兩個選項。

For our school trip, our class can visit either an art museum or an amusement park.
任務 2｜比較兩者差異

哪一句同時比較兩個選項？

比較句必須同時說明美術館和遊樂園的不同特色。

目前的內容
① For our school trip, our class can visit either an art museum or an amusement park.
任務 3｜選擇並說明理由

作者想選美術館，哪一句表達最完整？

這一句要同時包含「選擇什麼」和「為什麼」。

目前的內容
① For our school trip, our class can visit either an art museum or an amusement park.
② An art museum offers educational exhibits, while an amusement park provides exciting rides.
任務 4｜選出具體例子

哪一個例子最能支持第三句？

例子要具體說明在美術館可以如何進行課堂外的學習。

目前的內容
① For our school trip, our class can visit either an art museum or an amusement park.
② An art museum offers educational exhibits, while an amusement park provides exciting rides.
③ I would choose the art museum because I want to learn outside the classroom.

第6回｜四句話寫作

分享經驗與收穫

經驗 → 經過 → 感受 → 收穫

第 1 頁／共 3 頁
本頁題目示範題：旅行時迷路。這次經驗可以如何一步一步發展？
任務 1｜找出主要經驗

這一句主要交代什麼？

先判斷這句在經驗段落中的工作。

During a family trip last year, I got lost at a busy train station.
任務 2｜決定發展方向

哪一個英文句子最適合接在第一句後面？

三句都和這次旅行有關，請選出能交代「迷路後如何應對」的句子。

目前的內容
① During a family trip last year, I got lost at a busy train station.
任務 3｜第三句：寫出感受

哪一句最適合寫出事情結束後的感受？

第三句接著寫事情告一段落時，自己有什麼感受。

目前的內容
① During a family trip last year, I got lost at a busy train station.
② After looking around for a while, I stayed calm and asked a station worker for help.
任務 4｜第四句：說明收穫

哪一句最適合放在最後，寫出這次經驗帶來的收穫？

三句都像是在談收穫，請選出和前面經驗最符合的一句。

目前的內容
① During a family trip last year, I got lost at a busy train station.
② After looking around for a while, I stayed calm and asked a station worker for help.
③ When I finally found my family, I felt greatly relieved.
任務 5｜排出完整經驗

依照合理順序，依次點選四個句子。

點選下方字條，把句子依序放入四個位置。如果放錯，再點一次已放入的字條，就能讓它退回原來的位置。

1　經驗
2　經過
3　感受
4　收穫

① 經驗｜During a family trip last year, I got lost at a busy train station.

② 經過｜After looking around for a while, I stayed calm and asked a station worker for help.

③ 感受｜When I finally found my family, I felt greatly relieved.

④ 收穫｜From this experience, I learned that staying calm can help me deal with unexpected problems.

任務 6｜把中文想法寫成英文

練習題：第一次獨自搭公車

請先把四句答案寫在學習單或筆記本上。想不出來時再按「給我提示」，四句完成後再查看完整參考段落。

① 經驗

上星期六，我第一次獨自搭公車去圖書館。

提示一｜時間＋主詞＋交通方式＋目的地＋第一次
  • last Saturday
  • take the bus to the library
  • by myself
  • for the first time
  • 注意 take 的過去式
Last Saturday, I took the bus to the library by myself for the first time.參考答案不是唯一寫法，請比較自己的句子是否表達相同意思。

② 經過

搭車途中，我用手機查看行車路線，並仔細聽自己的下車站名。

提示一｜During the ride, I … and …
  • follow the route on my phone
  • listen carefully for my stop
  • 兩個主要動詞使用過去式
During the ride, I followed the route on my phone and listened carefully for my stop.參考答案不是唯一寫法，請比較自己的句子是否表達相同意思。

③ 感受

一開始我感到緊張，但抵達圖書館時，我為自己感到驕傲。

提示一｜At first, I felt …, but … when …
  • feel nervous
  • be proud of myself
  • reach the library
  • 使用過去式
At first, I felt nervous, but I was proud of myself when I reached the library.參考答案不是唯一寫法，請比較自己的句子是否表達相同意思。

④ 收穫

從這次經驗中，我明白充分準備能給我信心，讓我獨自嘗試新的事物。

提示一｜From this experience, I learned that …
  • careful preparation
  • give someone the confidence to …
  • try new things
  • on my own
From this experience, I learned that careful preparation can give me the confidence to try new things on my own.參考答案不是唯一寫法，請比較自己的句子是否表達相同意思。

四句都寫完了嗎？

先檢查時態與四句的連貫性，再查看完整參考段落。

Last Saturday, I took the bus to the library by myself for the first time.

During the ride, I followed the route on my phone and listened carefully for my stop.

At first, I felt nervous, but I was proud of myself when I reached the library.

From this experience, I learned that careful preparation can give me the confidence to try new things on my own.

請確認自己的內容依序包含：經驗、經過、感受與收穫。不同寫法只要意思合理，也可以成立。

任務 6｜連貫翻譯練習

換一個問題：學生花太多時間使用手機

作答方式

請先把四句英文寫在學習單或筆記本上。不知道怎麼寫時，再按「給我提示」；四句都完成後，最後查看完整參考段落。

① Problem｜問題

許多學生每天花太多時間使用手機。

第一層提示
  • many students
  • spend too much time...
第二層提示
  • on their phones
  • every day
Many students spend too much time on their phones every day.

② Cause｜原因

他們可能不斷查看訊息或觀看短片，而沒有注意到時間。

第一層提示
  • keep checking messages
  • watching short videos
第二層提示
  • without noticing the time
  • may + 原形動詞
They may keep checking messages or watching short videos without noticing the time.

③ Solution｜解決方法

他們可以設定使用時間限制，並在讀書時把手機收起來。

第一層提示
  • set a time limit
  • put their phones away
第二層提示
  • when they study
  • can + 原形動詞
They can set a time limit and put their phones away when they study.

④ Result｜結果

如此一來，他們可以更專心在重要的事情上，也能更有效地運用時間。

第一層提示
  • as a result
  • focus on important tasks
第二層提示
  • use their time more effectively
  • can + 原形動詞
As a result, they can focus on important tasks and use their time more effectively.

四句都寫完了嗎？

Many students spend too much time on their phones every day.

They may keep checking messages or watching short videos without noticing the time.

They can set a time limit and put their phones away when they study.

As a result, they can focus on important tasks and use their time more effectively.

參考答案不是唯一寫法。請檢查四句是否依序說明問題、原因、解決方法與結果。

任務 6｜換題練習

進入新學校，不知道如何交朋友

題目
Some students have difficulty making friends when they enter a new school. What can they do?

先判斷最適合的發展方式，再把四句英文寫在學習單或筆記本上。

① Problem｜問題

有些學生進入新學校時，不容易交到朋友。

第一層提示
  • have difficulty + V-ing
  • make friends
第二層提示
  • when they enter...
  • a new school
Some students have difficulty making friends when they enter a new school.

② Cause｜原因

他們可能感到害羞，不敢主動和別人交談。

第一層提示
  • feel shy
  • be afraid to...
第二層提示
  • start conversations
  • with others
They may feel shy and be afraid to start conversations with others.

③ Solution｜解決方法

他們可以向同學打招呼、詢問簡單的問題，並參加校園活動。

第一層提示
  • greet their classmates
  • ask simple questions
第二層提示
  • join school activities
  • A, B, and C
They can greet their classmates, ask simple questions, and join school activities.

④ Result｜結果

如此一來，他們可以逐漸認識新朋友，也能更自在地融入校園生活。

第一層提示
  • as a result
  • gradually make new friends
第二層提示
  • feel more comfortable
  • at school
As a result, they can gradually make new friends and feel more comfortable at school.

四句都寫完了嗎？

Some students have difficulty making friends when they enter a new school.

They may feel shy and be afraid to start conversations with others.

They can greet their classmates, ask simple questions, and join school activities.

As a result, they can gradually make new friends and feel more comfortable at school.

參考答案不是唯一寫法。請檢查內容是否依序回應問題、原因、解決方法與結果。
